# Code of Conduct

WorldForge welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.
Participation in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and project-related collaboration
requires respectful behavior.

Be considerate, give constructive technical feedback, acknowledge mistakes, and focus criticism
on ideas and code. Respect people's privacy and boundaries. Harassment, discriminatory remarks,
threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, and publishing private information without consent
are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, pause a discussion, reject a contribution,
or restrict participation to protect the community. Responses should be proportionate to the
behavior and may consider repeated violations. These expectations also apply to maintainers.

Report misconduct using a private contact route a maintainer has explicitly made available. If
none is available, ask in an issue for a private conduct-reporting channel without identifying
affected people or publishing private details. A dedicated contact address has not yet been
established. Reports should be handled discreetly, with information shared only as needed to
investigate and respond.
